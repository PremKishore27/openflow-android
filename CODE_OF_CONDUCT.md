# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OpenFlow to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone who uses or contributes to it, whatever their age, background, experience, identity or skill level.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub, Android or iOS.
- Give and accept feedback kindly. Criticise the code or the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intentions, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of spam or advertising.

## What is not okay

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sharing other people's private information, or anything that makes someone feel unsafe.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting (**Security → Report a vulnerability**) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
